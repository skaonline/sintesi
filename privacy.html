Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Sintesi records your voice, transcribes it and rewrites it in different forms. All processing happens on your phone, using AI models downloaded once and kept on the device. This page explains, without evasion, what stays on the phone and what leaves it.

What never leaves your phone

  • Your audio recordings.
  • Your transcriptions and every text generated or edited.
  • Your titles, tags and anything you type in the app.

There are no accounts, and this content is never uploaded anywhere. It lives in the app's private storage and disappears if you uninstall Sintesi.

When the app uses the network

  • To download the models (about 3 GB, the first time or when you add more). It is a plain file transfer: nothing of yours is sent.
  • To show ads, through Google AdMob — see below.
  • To send a technical report when something goes wrong — see Error reports.

Outside these three cases, the app works with no network at all.

Advertising

Sintesi shows a banner at the bottom of some screens, served by Google AdMob. To do so, Google receives technical data about the device: advertising identifier, IP address, device type and information about your interaction with the ad.

Google does not receive the content of your notes: not the audio, not the transcriptions, not the generated texts. The ad has no idea what you are talking about.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form managed by Google appears the first time you open the app. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ads and privacy inside the app. If you decline personalised ads you will still see ads, but they will not be profiled.

How Google handles this data is described in Google Privacy & Terms and in the list of advertising partners.

Usage analytics

None. We do not know how often you open the app, or what you do with it.

Error reports

If the app crashes or runs into an unexpected error, it sends a technical report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a service that collects error reports for developers. Reports are stored in the European Union, in Germany.

A report contains:

  • the error, and where in the app's code it happened;
  • the phone model, the Android version, the app version, the language, the time zone, and technical details such as available memory;
  • a short list of technical events just before the error — for example, which screen was open.

A report does not contain your recordings, the transcriptions, the text of your notes, titles or tags, what you tap on the screen, screenshots, your IP address (we have turned off storing it) or the advertising identifier.

Reports are used only to find and fix bugs, are not shared with anyone else, and are deleted automatically by Sentry after at most 90 days.

Permissions

  • Microphone — to record. Only while recording.
  • Notifications — to tell you how a transcription is going.
  • Foreground service — to keep recording or processing when you leave the app, so Android does not interrupt the work.
  • Network — for the two cases described above.

Keeping and deleting

It is up to you. A deleted note goes to the bin and is removed permanently after 30 days; you can empty the bin sooner. From Settings you can export everything or free up space. Uninstalling the app deletes recordings, texts and models with it.

Children

Sintesi is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Substantial changes will be announced in the app.
